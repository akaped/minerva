A few things worth knowing.

Is Minerva free?

Minerva is free, open-source software under the MIT License. You can use, modify, and share it. You provide the computer or server it runs on. Hosting and optional AI providers may have their own costs.

Can I just sign up and start?

There isn’t a hosted Minerva signup service. You can run your own instance or build the Windows/Linux desktop app. The getting-started guide explains your options.

Do I need AI to use it?

No. Notes, journals, links, tables, collaboration, and exports work without AI. If you enable an AI feature, the chosen content is sent to the provider you configure. You decide whether that fits your work.

Does it work offline?

The desktop app supports personal notes and offline copies of server workspaces. Browser collaboration is online; unsent browser edits are not durably queued across page reloads. Keep that distinction in mind when choosing how to work.

Is it ready for my whole organization?

Minerva is an early-access project suited to evaluation and small workspaces. Large-scale deployments and stronger operational guarantees need more work. Back up your data, review the limitations, and help us improve it with real-world feedback.

Can I help if I don’t code?

Absolutely. Test a workflow, improve documentation, share an original template, or give thoughtful design feedback. Start with the contribution guide. Small improvements count.
